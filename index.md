I'm an applied development economist, with a particular focus on technology, labour, education, and policy macroeconomics, including structural transformation and aid effectiveness.

I work as a Senior Research Fellow with [UNU-WIDER](https://www.wider.unu.edu) based in Mozambique at the Ministry of Planning & Development (Maputo), where I lead the [Inclusive Growth in Mozambique](https://igmozambique.wider.unu.edu/) programme. I'm also an [Associate Professor](https://www.economics.ku.dk/staff/vip/?pure=en/persons/335694) at the University of Copenhagen, Department of Economics (on leave for public service).
