I'm an applied development economist, with a particular focus on technology, labour, education, and policy macroeconomics, including structural transformation and aid effectiveness.

I work as a Senior Research Fellow with [UNU-WIDER](https://www.wider.unu.edu) in Mozambique, based at the Ministry of Planning & Development (Maputo), where I lead the [Inclusive Growth in Mozambique](https://igmozambique.wider.unu.edu/) programme. I'm also an [Associate Professor](https://www.economics.ku.dk/staff/vip/?pure=en/persons/335694) at the University of Copenhagen, Department of Economics, with the [Development Economics Research Group](https://www.econ.ku.dk/derg/) (on leave for  public service). I've worked with the government of Mozambique for over 13 years in their economic studies and macroeconomic analysis division, based in Maputo. 

This is my __personal website__, where you can find my [academic publications](https://esamjones.github.io/publications/), general writing and so on.

Enjoy browsing this site, or get in contact with me if you have any questions/comments.
